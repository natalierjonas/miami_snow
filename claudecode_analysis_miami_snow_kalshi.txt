⏺ It's a reporting trick. Kalshi counts volume by notional value — each contract
   is worth $1 if it pays out.

  The math:
  - 587,689 contracts were traded, each with a $1 face value = ~$588K notional
  - But people only paid 1–2 cents per contract on average
  - Actual money that changed hands: $9,435

  So Kalshi says "$480K+ volume" because that's the theoretical payout if every
  contract hit. In reality, traders only spent $9,435 total — roughly 62x less
  than the headline number. It's like saying a $1 scratch-off lottery ticket
  represents "$10,000 in volume" because that's the jackpot.

  The $480K vs our $588K gap is likely because Kalshi counts contracts once (one
   side of the trade), while our scrape may double-count some resales, or the
  Feb market is still active and Kalshi's number is a snapshot from a different
  time.
